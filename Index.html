About the Series

School Bus Graveyard is a thriller Webtoon created by Red. It follows Ashlyn Banner and her classmates as they are dragged into a dangerous alternate world that awakens every night at midnight.

The story takes place in Alto, Georgia around 2016, beginning near the start of the school year.

Series Information

Premiere: July 20, 2021 (Webtoon Originals)

Genre: Thriller

Rating: 9.9 / 10

Popularity: Over 135M views and nearly 2M subscribers
