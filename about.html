School Bus Graveyard
What Is School Bus Graveyard?

School Bus Graveyard is a thriller Webtoon created by Red. The story follows Ashlyn Banner and her classmates as they are pulled into a dangerous alternate world that awakens every night at midnight.

The series originally began as a Canvas comic before being promoted to Webtoon Originals on July 20, 2021.

Setting

The story takes place in Alto, Georgia, around the year 2016. Much of the plot revolves around the mysterious “phantom dimension,” a twisted version of reality filled with monsters and corrupted versions of familiar places.

Themes

School Bus Graveyard blends:

  • Thriller and suspense
  • Found family
  • Survival horror
  • Character‑driven storytelling
  • Mystery and world‑building
Popularity

The series has earned:

  • Over 135 million views
  • Nearly 2 million subscribers
  • A 9.9/10 rating on Webtoon
